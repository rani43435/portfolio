Glance Pay

Step inside ↗

Recorded in the Meta Ray-Ban Display simulator over four backgrounds: a bridge, a café, a train station at night and the bare display.

Press the card to switch between Visa and Mastercard. Scan to pay opens the camera and finds the shop's code. The amount appears; tilt your head right to pay, or press Pay. Every payment lands in History, kept on the glasses.

About

Self-taught, and shipping.

I'm Rani Marjy, a VR and AR developer. I taught myself Unity, C# and Blender by building things, and I now study Augmented & Virtual Reality Design (AVRD) at Hochschule Darmstadt.

I like projects that leave the screen: a companion that stands next to you, a video that spills light onto your wall, a payment you make with a glance.
